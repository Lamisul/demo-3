01 / ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Engineer by training.

I have an academic background in Mechanical Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My interests include robotics, artificial intelligence, mechatronics, and the development of practical engineering solutions.

My undergraduate thesis focused on the development of a laboratory-scale paper production system using water hyacinth fiber. This work brought together machine design, fabrication, and experimental investigation.

I am now looking to deepen my research capabilities and explore intelligent, autonomous, and interdisciplinary engineering systems.
